Technical Overview

The AI monitoring layer behind every reactor.

Neer Sentinel's passive magnetic core does the separation. Alongside it, a continuous sensing and AI layer watches every stage of the cycle — turning raw reactor telemetry into predictive maintenance, conversational insight, and audit-ready compliance records.

The Concept

Inline sensing, not a bolt-on network.

Rather than scattering sensor nodes across a facility, Neer Sentinel's array lives inside the reactor itself — reading the water at every stage it passes through.

01

Inlet

Raw effluent is read for flow rate, pH, turbidity and TDS the moment it enters the unit, before any treatment begins.

02

Magnetic Matrix

Sensors track how much pollutant load the matrix has captured, so backwash cycles are triggered by actual saturation, not a fixed timer.

03

RO & Outlet

Membrane pressure and treated-water quality are checked against the inlet reading to confirm every cycle meets discharge targets.
